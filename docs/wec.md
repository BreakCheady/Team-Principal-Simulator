# FIA WEC replacement

WEC replaces the former historical LMP1 selection. Both Hypercar and LMGT3 are
playable through the team selector. The game has nine fictional teams per class,
two car entries per team and three employed drivers per car. LMGT3 budgets and car
pace are calibrated separately from Hypercar.

## Reference profile

The [2026 FIA WEC sporting regulations](https://www.fia.com/system/files/documents/2026_fia_world_endurance_championship_sporting_regulations_clean_v1.2wmsc.pdf)
provide the selected rules: top-ten Hyperpole separately per class, Bronze-only
LMGT3 qualifying, no Bronze Hypercar drivers, LMGT3 Bronze plus Bronze/Silver crews,
minimum driving times and class points. Six-hour races award 25–18–15–12–10–8–6–4–2–1;
Le Mans doubles race points. Pole awards one point per class. Finish classification
requires crossing the finish and 70% of the overall leader's distance.

LMGT3 Bronze/Silver minimum driving time is 105 minutes over six hours. With two
Silver drivers, one must meet that minimum. All drivers need 45 minutes for points.
Le Mans uses six hours for Bronze/Silver, following the
[2026 supplementary regulations](https://fiawec.alkamelsystems.com/Results_NoticeBoard/14_2026/committee/00_Regulations/2026%20-%20Le%20Mans%20-%20Supplementary%20Regulation%20V2.pdf).
Automated pit plans rotate crews to meet these targets. Refuelling and tyre service
are sequential in the pit model.

Hypercar manufacturer points sum the two designated cars. LMGT3 team trophies
score each persistent car entry separately. Driver standings are split by class;
eligible crew members share car points. Management assessment still sums a team's
two entries, within the player's class.

## Saves and scope

Save version 11 upgrades versions 6–10. Existing LMP1 teams become Hypercar teams;
new WEC entries start on zero points. IDs, contracts, finances, political history
and progress are preserved. Active legacy weekends retain their original venue,
duration and clock, then use the new calendar from the next event.

This is a selected sporting profile with fictional competitors. It does not model
all official regulations: rolling driving-time maxima, tyre allocations, detailed
BoP/energy systems, guest LMP2 entries at Le Mans, event-specific exceptions and
stewards' discretionary decisions remain simplified. Track geometry and lap pace
are procedural game values. Drivers' ratings are persistent game attributes.
